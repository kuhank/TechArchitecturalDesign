CLAIMS (indicative, for the complete specification)

I claim:

1.  A system for selective chaos control of an autonomous agent, comprising at least one processor and a memory storing instructions which, when executed, cause the processor to: generate a plurality of meaning-preserving perturbations of an input; obtain a candidate output of the agent for each perturbation; decompose each candidate output into a plurality of output dimensions, each having a distance measure and a role selected from a precision role and an exploratory role; compute for each dimension a divergence and a finite-step exponent relative to a perturbation magnitude; apply to each precision dimension whose divergence lies outside a target band the lowest-cost intervention from an ordered intervention set whose predicted error meets a tolerance, while accepting a consensus value for precision dimensions within the band; maintain the divergence of each exploratory dimension within a band by adjusting a dimension-specific variability parameter; and compose a final output from the selected dimension values.

2.  The system of claim 1, wherein the ordered intervention set comprises consensus, grounded regeneration of only the affected dimension, external verification, and escalation or safe stop.

3.  The system of claim 1, wherein the processor computes for each dimension an estimated error-rate range from its divergence as [1 − √(1 − d), ½ (1 − √(1 − 2d))].

4.  The system of claim 1, wherein the variability parameter is updated by a log-proportional feedback law whose gain is kept below a critical gain 2/(τ^(⋆)g′(τ^(⋆))) computed from recent parameter–divergence pairs.

5.  The system of claim 1, further comprising a horizon manager that estimates an error-growth rate of a multi-step agent from a series of deviations and triggers re-validation at a fraction α < 1 of the predictability horizon λ⁻¹ln (Δ/e₀).

6.  The system of claim 1, further comprising a master controller that checks constraints between dimensions, re-processes violating dimensions, and outputs a stability index computed from the weighted divergences.

7.  An autonomous robot comprising sensors, actuators, a planner and the system of claim 1, wherein safety-related dimensions are assigned the precision role and route-, strategy- or approach-related dimensions are assigned the exploratory role, and wherein only an action composed from admissible dimension values is passed to the actuators.

8.  A computer-implemented method for selective chaos control of an autonomous agent, comprising the steps performed by the processor in any of claims 1 to 6.
